Build a beautiful foundation

A good makeup routine begins with preparation, clean tools and a light hand. You can learn the fundamentals without buying a full collection.

  • Cleanse and moisturize before makeup
  • Learn what each brush and sponge does
  • Choose products for your skin needs
  • Apply in thin layers and blend patiently
  • Remove makeup gently at the end of the day
